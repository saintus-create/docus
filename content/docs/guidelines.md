- **WHO Essential Medicines**: Long-acting stimulants hold inclusion status in the 2023 World Health Organization Essential Medicines List.

- **Global First-Line Therapy**: Lisdexamfetamine serves as first-line treatment across United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and Germany regulatory frameworks.

- **Efficacy Evidence**: Systematic reviews identify stimulant pharmacotherapy as demonstrating the highest effect size in psychiatric treatment.
