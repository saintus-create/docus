# 3. Ethics and Good Clinical Practices


### 3.1 Submission Quality and Integrity

    Review of the quality of the submitted data, including completeness, accuracy, and adherence to regulatory standards.


### 3.2 Compliance with Good Clinical Practices

    Verification of GCP compliance during clinical trials, including IRB oversight, informed consent, and protocol adherence.


### 3.3 Financial Disclosures

    Analysis of financial interests of investigators to assess potential conflicts of interest that could affect study integrity.
